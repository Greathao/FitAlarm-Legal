FitAlarm

隐私政策

我们尽量少收集数据。动作识别在你的设备上完成，摄像头画面不会上传到我们的服务器。

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 9 日

本政策适用于 FitAlarm iOS 应用及本支持网站。

1. 我们是谁

FitAlarm（以下简称“我们”）是一款通过闹钟和动作挑战帮助用户起床、行动并培养习惯的 iOS 应用。

2. 我们处理的信息

摄像头与人体姿态

当你主动进入动作挑战时，FitAlarm 会请求摄像头权限，并使用 Apple Vision 在设备本地分析画面中的人体关键点，以判断动作阶段和完成次数。摄像头画面、视频和人体姿态数据不会由我们保存或上传，也不会用于身份识别。

闹钟、目标与偏好

闹钟时间、重复规则、动作目标、连续记录、设置与完成状态保存在你的设备上，用于提供应用功能。删除应用可能同时删除这些本地数据。

购买信息

订阅和永久购买由 Apple App Store 处理。我们不会收到或保存你的银行卡完整信息。应用可通过 StoreKit 获取购买状态、产品标识和权益状态，以解锁 Pro 功能及恢复购买。

支持沟通

如果你通过电子邮件或 GitHub Issues 联系我们，我们会收到你主动提供的信息，例如邮箱地址、设备与系统信息、问题描述及附件，并仅将其用于回复和解决问题。

3. 权限的用途

  • 摄像头：仅在动作挑战期间进行设备端动作计数。
  • 闹钟：通过 Apple AlarmKit 创建、显示和管理你设置的闹钟。
  • 通知（如请求）：提供与闹钟、提醒或应用状态有关的信息。

你可以随时在 iOS“设置”中更改权限。关闭某项权限会使相关功能不可用，但不会影响其他不依赖该权限的功能。

4. 数据共享与跟踪

当前版本不要求注册账户，不包含第三方广告 SDK，也不使用第三方分析 SDK进行跨应用或跨网站跟踪。我们不出售、出租或交换你的个人信息。

Apple 可能依据其自身政策处理 App Store 购买、系统诊断、闹钟和权限相关数据。相关处理由 Apple 的条款与隐私政策约束。

5. 数据保留与删除

应用功能数据主要保留在你的设备上，直到你在应用内清除、重置相关数据或删除应用。支持沟通记录仅在处理请求、维护服务记录或履行法律义务所需期间保留。你可通过支持页面联系我们，请求删除你曾直接提交给我们的个人信息。

6. 儿童隐私

FitAlarm 并非专门面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集其个人信息。如果你认为儿童向我们提交了个人信息，请联系我们处理删除。

7. 安全与跨境处理

我们采取与应用规模和数据性质相适应的措施保护信息，但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。若你通过电子邮件或 GitHub 联系我们，信息可能由相应服务提供商在其运营地区处理。

8. 政策变更

应用功能、法律要求或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并修改生效日期。重大变化将通过合理方式提示。

9. 联系我们

对本政策或个人信息处理有疑问，请前往支持中心联系我们。

Privacy Policy

We minimize data collection. Exercise recognition runs on your device, and camera footage is not uploaded to our servers.

Effective: August 9, 2026

This policy applies to the FitAlarm iOS app and this support website.

1. About FitAlarm

FitAlarm ("we," "us," or "our") is an iOS app designed to help users wake up, take action, and build habits through alarms and movement challenges.

2. Information We Process

Camera and body pose

When you start a movement challenge, FitAlarm requests camera access and uses Apple Vision to analyze body landmarks on your device so it can estimate movement stages and completed repetitions. We do not save or upload camera frames, videos, or body-pose data, and we do not use them to identify you.

Alarms, goals, and preferences

Alarm times, repeat rules, movement goals, streaks, settings, and completion status are stored on your device to provide app features. Deleting the app may also delete this local data.

Purchase information

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. Through StoreKit, the app may access product identifiers, entitlement status, and purchase status to unlock Pro features and restore purchases.

Support communications

If you contact us by email or GitHub Issues, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, device and system details, issue description, and attachments. We use it only to respond and resolve the issue.

3. Permission Use

  • Camera: on-device repetition counting during a movement challenge.
  • Alarms: creating, presenting, and managing alarms you schedule through Apple AlarmKit.
  • Notifications, if requested: information related to alarms, reminders, or app status.

You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Disabling a permission makes the related feature unavailable but does not affect features that do not depend on it.

4. Sharing and Tracking

The current version does not require an account, include third-party advertising SDKs, or use third-party analytics SDKs for tracking across apps or websites. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Apple may process App Store purchases, system diagnostics, alarm data, and permission information under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Retention and Deletion

App feature data remains primarily on your device until you clear or reset it in the app or delete the app. We retain support communications only as long as needed to handle your request, maintain service records, or meet legal obligations. Contact us through the support page to request deletion of personal information you submitted directly to us.

6. Children's Privacy

FitAlarm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information so we can address deletion.

7. Security and International Processing

We use measures appropriate to the app's size and the nature of the information, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. If you contact us by email or GitHub, those providers may process information in the regions where they operate.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date. We will provide reasonable notice of material changes.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this policy or personal-information handling, contact us through the Support Center.
